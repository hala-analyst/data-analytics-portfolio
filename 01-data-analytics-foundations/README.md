## 2. What Is Data Analytics?
Data analytics is the process of examining data to identify useful information, recognize patterns, and support better decision-making.

Businesses use data analytics to understand performance, identify problems, and make more informed decisions.

## 3. Why Is Data Analytics Important?
Data analytics can help organizations:
- Understand customer needs
- Monitor business performance
- Identify trends and patterns
- Evaluate business decisions
- Discover opportunities for improvement
